Common questions

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aidentika → Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app or the account does not cancel it.

How do I restore purchases?

In the app open Profile → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app open Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your projects, photos and results.
